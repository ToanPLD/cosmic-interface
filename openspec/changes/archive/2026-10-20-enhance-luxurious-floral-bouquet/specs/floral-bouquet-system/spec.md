# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: High-Fidelity Botanical Petal Anatomy
The system SHALL render individual flower petals with compound Gaussian 3D curvature, procedural radiating micro-vein texture striations, and a 4-stop soft radial color gradient from cream to rose.

#### Scenario: Petal procedural texture and curvature inspection
- **WHEN** individual rose petals are examined during orbit or within the assembled flower heads
- **THEN** petals exhibit visible radiating veins from base to tip, smooth gradient coloration (`#fef3c7` base through `#fce7f3` and `#f472b6` to `#e11d48` outer margin), and a compound spoon dish curvature with curled reflexed edges

### Requirement: Dense Interlocking English Rose Architecture
The system SHALL construct rose blossom heads with at least 30 interlocking concentric petals organized across four concentric botanical whorls, eliminating visible gaps into the blossom core.

#### Scenario: Rose blossom density and whorl nesting
- **WHEN** rose flower heads are rendered in the assembled bouquet
- **THEN** each rose contains at least 30 petals layered through a central scroll core (6 petals), inner cup whorl (8 petals), mid whorl (10 petals), and reflexed outer base (8 petals) with zero hollow gaps visible from any angle

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Convergent Vortex Assembly Phase
The system SHALL transition orbiting petals into logarithmic spiral trajectories toward the center origin and interpolate them into accurate blossom coordinates.

#### Scenario: Inward golden vortex movement
- **WHEN** the animation timeline progresses from 4.0 to 8.0 seconds
- **THEN** high-detail curved petals contract along golden ratio spiral pathways toward center origin, smoothly orienting their compound curvatures to dock into dense 32-petal English garden roses, ruffled carnations, and perimeter florets

### Requirement: Wrapping and Ribbon Coiling Phase
The system SHALL animate wrapping paper panels around the flower stems and coil a burgundy satin ribbon into a decorative bow with a greeting card display.

#### Scenario: Bouquet packaging and ribbon attachment
- **WHEN** the animation timeline reaches 8.0 to 11.0 seconds
- **THEN** a continuous parametric pleated origami wrapping paper mesh sweeps around the stems with soft sinusoidal folds, an inner crinkled silk ruffle collar extends to cradle the blossoms, a burgundy satin ribbon ties into a bow, and a "LUCKY TO HAVE YOU" greeting card displays smoothly
