# Spec Delta: floral-bouquet-system

## Purpose

Provides a procedural 3D floral bouquet assembly experience where dispersed orbiting petals and botanical elements spiral inward to construct an elegant flower gift with wrapping paper and ribbon.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Dispersed Petal Orbital Phase
The system SHALL maintain an empty central focal point during the initial phase while individual 3D flower petals, florets, and leaves circulate along inclined 3D orbital trajectories.

#### Scenario: Initial scene loading and orbit flight
- **WHEN** the animation timeline begins between 0.0 and 4.0 seconds
- **THEN** the center origin remains empty, and individual rose petals, carnation petals, blue florets, and eucalyptus leaves orbit in 3D space with continuous flutter and spin

### Requirement: Convergent Vortex Assembly Phase
The system SHALL transition orbiting petals into logarithmic spiral trajectories toward the center origin and interpolate them into accurate blossom coordinates.

#### Scenario: Inward golden vortex movement
- **WHEN** the animation timeline progresses from 4.0 to 8.0 seconds
- **THEN** petal orbital radii contract smoothly toward center, and elements dock into place forming layered rose centers, ruffled carnations, and perimeter florets

### Requirement: Wrapping and Ribbon Coiling Phase
The system SHALL animate wrapping paper panels around the flower stems and coil a burgundy satin ribbon into a decorative bow with a greeting card display.

#### Scenario: Bouquet packaging and ribbon attachment
- **WHEN** the animation timeline reaches 8.0 to 11.0 seconds
- **THEN** layered blush wrapping paper panels sweep inward to enclose the stems, a burgundy ribbon wraps into a bow, and a "LUCKY TO HAVE YOU" card appears

### Requirement: Complete Bouquet Presentation and Continuous Orbit
The system SHALL present the completed floral bouquet with soft emissive bloom, floating fairy sparkles, and 360-degree orbital camera navigation.

#### Scenario: Completed bouquet inspection
- **WHEN** the timeline exceeds 11.0 seconds
- **THEN** the fully assembled bouquet remains stably rendered with gentle ambient sway, fairy dust particles float upward, and the camera performs a smooth 360-degree orbit with pointer interaction enabled
